# Principles

These principles are advisory. They are consulted by reflection when no
convention fires; the agent itself only acts on structured rules.

## OpenWithValues

Open the bidding when you hold 12+ HCP, or 11 HCP with a good 6-card suit.
A passed-out hand with 22+ combined HCP is a partnership failure: someone
should have opened.

## NaturalLongest

When opening 1 of a suit, prefer your longest suit. With equal length
between two 5-card suits, open the higher-ranking. With 4-4 in the majors
and minimum, prefer the 4-card heart suit if and only if the system says
so — otherwise the opening table decides.

## RaiseWithFit

With three or more cards in partner's natural suit and 6+ HCP, raise.
Limit raises (10-12 HCP, 3+ trumps) are preferable to ambiguous new suits.

## NTRequiresBalanced

Do not open or rebid notrump without a balanced or semi-balanced hand.

## SymbolicOverNatural

When a convention assigns a precise meaning to a bid, the convention wins
over a natural reading. Partner is presumed to know the system.

## WhenInDoubtPass

If no convention matches and partner has not promised values, prefer Pass.
Symbolic communication is more valuable than guessing.
